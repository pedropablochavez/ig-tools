# Privacy Policy

**HD Profile Picture Viewer** does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.

- **No data collection.** The extension has no analytics, telemetry, tracking, or remote server.
- **No network requests of its own.** It only reads, inside your own browser tab, the profile picture URLs
  that the website already loaded for you.
- **No storage.** Picture URLs are kept in memory in the current tab only and are discarded when the tab
  is closed or reloaded.
- **Downloads.** When you click **Download**, the image is saved to your computer using your browser's
  download feature. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `https://www.instagram.com/*` | To show the button and viewer on profile pages. |
| `downloads` | To save a picture to your computer when you click **Download**. |
