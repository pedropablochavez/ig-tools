# Privacy Policy

**IG Tools (Unofficial)** does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.

- **No data collection.** The extension has no analytics, telemetry, tracking, or remote server.
- **No network requests of its own.** It only reads, inside your own browser tab, the profile picture, post
  and story media URLs that the website already loaded for you. The only file it fetches is the one you click
  to download, directly from Instagram's servers. For the music feature it may also load, in a hidden frame,
  the Instagram profile of the author of a photo post you stop on: the same page you would see by opening it.
- **No storage.** Media URLs are kept in memory in the current tab only and are discarded when the tab
  is closed or reloaded.
- **Downloads.** When you click **Download**, the file is saved to your computer using your browser's
  download feature. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `https://www.instagram.com/*` | To show the buttons and viewer on profiles, posts and stories. |
| `downloads` | To save a file to your computer when you click a download button. |
| `declarativeNetRequest` | To let its own hidden profile frames (only those) load inside instagram.com. |
