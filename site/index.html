No tracking attached.

No analytics, ads or developer servers. The extension works with media the site provides to your browser. Photos, videos and music load from the original media servers.

Music and downloads use data. Temporary caches have limits, and playback stops when you leave the post or hide the tab.

Read the privacy policy ↗

Good to know.

Why do I need Developer mode?

This version is distributed as an unpacked extension. A normal website cannot directly install it. Once there is a Chrome Web Store listing, this page can link to the browser's standard installation flow, which also works in Brave.

Does it work with private accounts?

Only with content that your own session can already access. It does not unlock private accounts or bypass access restrictions.

How do I update or remove it?

For an update, replace the files in your existing installation folder with the new ZIP contents, click Reload on the extension card and refresh Instagram. To uninstall, click Remove on that card.

Is this an official Instagram extension?

No. IG Tools is an independent, unofficial project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram or Meta. Use downloaded content responsibly and respect its owners' rights.
